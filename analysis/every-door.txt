## Every door, and how a run reaches it

Regenerate with:
  cargo test -p gearmaster-engine --test validity -- --ignored --nocapture \
      report_every_door_and_what_it_wants

Under each answer: `->` is what the player is told it does, which is
`Outcome::describe` and is what a tooltip shows before it is taken.
`[...]` is the mechanical truth where the player-facing line hides it -
a silent counter says "nothing you could point to", which is the whole
of that mechanic and exactly wrong for a reference.

What an answer *did*, with the run's own numbers in it, is
`Run::receipt`: a bounty depends on the rung and a gamble on the roll,
and neither is knowable from here.

## THE HUNDRED, tile by tile

A county event stands on a tile rather than in front of a fight. Eight of
the nine are dealt onto eleven of the county's tiles - every one of them
once before any of them twice - and the ninth is the pale, which the
generator places. A tile is walked onto during a trip: five moves, ten
trips a run, and what you clear stays cleared for the rest of it.

None of them fights. The county's only fights are its three pinnacles
and THE PARISH, and `county::county_events_never_fight` is what keeps
that true.


THE PALE  [the-pale]
  stands: placed, not dealt: off the edge and off the circuit (V5), at least two tiles from every gate, one a county
    - Hand over an orb and open it           Requires: six tiles in each third of the county, two boundary stones, and an Orb of Travel
        -> The gatepost takes the orb
        -> Noted: the pale is open
           [takes an Orb-kind piece, worn or loose]
