GEAR MASTER
===========

Build gear out of parts, then watch your character fight.

  1. Unzip the folder somewhere (Downloads is fine)
  2. Run GearMaster.exe
  3. Drag pieces from the tray at the bottom into the five slots up top
  4. Press BEGIN FIGHT

Controls
  left-drag ......... move a piece
  right-click ....... rotate it
  drag to the tray .. take a piece back off
  Esc ............... cancel a drag
  AUTO-BUILD ........ fill every slot instantly, if you'd rather just watch

Pieces have to TOUCH to become a finished item. A gold outline means it came
together; a red one means it's still missing something. You can fit more than
one finished item in a slot, as long as they don't touch each other.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------
FIRST LAUNCH: WINDOWS WILL WARN YOU. Here's how to get past it.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

You'll see a blue box: "Windows protected your PC".

  1. Click "More info" (the small link, easy to miss)
  2. Click "Run anyway"

That's it — once only.

WHY: SmartScreen warns about any program it hasn't seen many people run
before. Getting rid of the warning means buying a code-signing certificate,
which costs a few hundred dollars a year. The warning is about the absence of
that certificate, not about anything found in the program. Only click through
it for software from someone you actually trust.

If your antivirus quarantines it instead, that's the same story — small
unsigned indie builds trip heuristics constantly. You may need to allow it
explicitly.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------
NOTES

Needs 64-bit Windows 10 or 11.

It's a single self-contained .exe. It doesn't install anything, doesn't need
the network, and doesn't write outside its own folder. To uninstall, delete
it.

If it opens and closes instantly, your graphics drivers may be too old — the
game needs OpenGL 3.3.
