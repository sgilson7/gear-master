GEAR MASTER
===========

Build gear out of parts, then watch your character fight.

  1. Unzip GearMaster-macOS.zip
  2. Drag pieces from the tray at the bottom into the five slots up top
  3. Press BEGIN FIGHT

Controls
  left-drag ......... move a piece
  right-click ....... rotate it
  drag to the tray .. take a piece back off
  Esc ............... cancel a drag
  AUTO-BUILD ........ fill every slot instantly, if you'd rather just watch

Pieces have to TOUCH to become a finished item. A gold outline means it came
together; a red one means it's still missing something. You can fit more than
one finished item in a slot, as long as they don't touch each other.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------
FIRST LAUNCH: macOS WILL BLOCK IT. Here's how to get past that.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

This is a hobby app that hasn't been through Apple's $99/year notarisation,
so macOS refuses to open it the first time. That's expected. It happens to
every app that isn't from the App Store or a paying developer.

  macOS 15 Sequoia and newer (including macOS 26)
  -----------------------------------------------
  1. Double-click GearMaster. You'll get "Apple could not verify..." — click
     Done. (Not "Move to Trash".)
  2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security
  3. Scroll to the bottom. There's a line about GearMaster being blocked,
     with an "Open Anyway" button. Click it.
  4. Confirm with Touch ID or your password.
  5. It opens. You only have to do this once.

  macOS 11 through 14
  -------------------
  1. RIGHT-CLICK (or Control-click) GearMaster, choose Open
  2. Click Open in the dialog
  Once only.

  Terminal shortcut, any version
  ------------------------------
  If you'd rather do it in one command, run this and then open it normally:

      xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /path/to/GearMaster.app

  (Drag the app onto the Terminal window to fill in the path.)


-------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY IS IT DOING THAT?

macOS flags anything downloaded from the internet and refuses to run apps it
can't trace to a paying Apple developer. The warning is about the absence of
a signature, not about anything found in the app. Only take this route for
software from someone you actually trust.

The app is a single self-contained file. It doesn't install anything, doesn't
need the network, and doesn't write anywhere outside itself. To uninstall,
drag it to the Trash.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------
"IT SAYS IT'S DAMAGED / WON'T OPEN AT ALL"

Some browsers and chat clients strip the executable bit. Fix with:

    chmod +x /path/to/GearMaster.app/Contents/MacOS/GearMaster

If it still won't start, you may be on an Intel Mac and this build may be
Apple Silicon only — ask whoever sent it to rebuild with the Intel slice
included.
